Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About My Work

As a Product Manager at Adobe, I work closely with the creative community, photographers, and designers to help them craft more efficient imaging workflows. I focus primarily on Adobe Photoshop, and Lightroom, providing support, creating educational content, and gathering feedback to help improve these products. I also speak at events, create tutorials, and engage with the community through various channels.

Start with the basics! I recommend beginning with understanding layers, masks, and essential tools. Check out my getting started guide on the blog, or explore the Adobe XD tutorials I've created. Practice regularly, and don't be afraid to experiment. You can also find many resources on Adobe.com.

I work with a variety of camera equipment depending on the project. For my landscape and travel photography, I focus on capturing the moment with whatever tools are available. The most important aspect is understanding light, composition, and post-processing techniques in Adobe Lightroom. Check out my Lightroom workflow tips to see how I process my images.
