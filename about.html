At the intersection of technology and creativity

I'm Matt Rae, a Product Manager at Adobe focused on Photoshop and Lightroom. I work at the intersection of technology and creativity, helping photographers and designers craft more efficient imaging workflows and bring their vision to life.

I've spent my career in product design and management roles across travel, education, autonomous vehicles, and marketing tech. I've built learning hubs for products like Adobe XD, and now focus on imaging and photography workflows that help creators work more efficiently.

When I'm not building products, I'm behind a camera—capturing landscapes, skyscapes, and travel photography. I also speak at design conferences and workshops, sharing insights on Adobe products, creative workflows, and product management.
