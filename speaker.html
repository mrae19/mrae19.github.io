About Matt Rae - Adobe Product Manager & Conference Speaker

Matt Rae is a Product Manager at Adobe, specializing in Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Lightroom. With over 10 years of experience in product design, product management, and creative technology, Matt brings a unique perspective to speaking engagements, combining technical expertise with real-world insights from working directly with photographers, designers, and creative professionals.

As an Adobe Product Manager, Matt has deep expertise in Adobe Creative Suite tools, particularly Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Lightroom. His presentations blend practical workflows, product strategy insights, and community-driven approaches to design and creativity. Whether discussing Adobe tools, product management best practices, creative workflows, or the future of AI agents and assistants in creative work, Matt delivers engaging talks that resonate with both technical and creative audiences.
